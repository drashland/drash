# Standard

Standalone library code, in the spirit of Deno's or Go's standard library. Standard imports only [Core](/reference/core) and is imported by [Modules](/reference/modules).

This is where the default implementations live: the chain handlers, the `Middleware` class, the resource group builder, the loggers, and the `URLPattern` polyfill.

## What You Actually Import

Almost nothing here, in a normal app. `Middleware` and `ResourceGroup` reach you through the HTTP module — `Middleware` is re-exported directly, and `ResourceGroup` through [`Resource.group()`](/reference/core/resource#resourcegroup).

The rest is here for the two cases where you go past the defaults: building a chain of your own out of `Handler` subclasses, and supplying your own `URLPattern` implementation.
